Rev. Dr. Marilyn White provided leadership for the development of a $33 million affordable senior housing community in Houston — work carried out before WW Housing Development was formed, and the kind of project the firm now advises on.

It is set out here as a worked example of what a project page looks like: what the site was, how it was funded, who carried which part of it, and what the congregation was left holding at the end.

What the project involved

A development of this size runs on three things at once — a site that can physically carry the units, a funding structure that clears at the affordability target, and someone holding the City of Houston process together while both of those move.

What we would bring to a project like this

The same sequence the firm runs on every engagement, in plain language, with a straight answer at each stage about whether the project still works.

  • A route to the money, mapped

    LIHTC, City of Houston programmes, HUD routes and private capital assessed against what the site and the organisation can actually carry.

  • Approvals carried, not handed over

    Submission and sequencing through the City of Houston, with one named liaison so nothing sits waiting on an unowned task.

  • One point of contact to close

    Construction monitored against the plan and closings coordinated, with the congregation keeping an advocate at the table until residents move in.
