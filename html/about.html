About Us

A firm built for the congregations it serves

WW Housing Development LLC exists for one reason: churches and community groups across Houston are sitting on land that could become housing, and almost none of them have been given a straight, plain-language path to building it.

The Firm

Why this firm, and why now

Many Houston churches own land or buildings they no longer fully use. They hold something a developer cannot buy, which is deep trust in their own neighbourhood. What they lack is experience with development financing, tax credits, and government programmes. No consulting firm in this city currently serves that gap in a clear, accessible way. We were formed to fill it.

Guide churches, community groups, and landowners through affordable housing development in plain language, from the first look at a property to the day residents move in.

Houston congregations using land they already own to create housing their own neighbourhoods can afford, without handing control of the outcome to an outside developer.

Consulting and navigation as standard, with co-development where it fits, so clients keep an advocate who has a direct stake in how the project turns out.

  • Legal and tax structuring

    Church property transactions, closings, and tax credit deals handled in house.

  • Ministry and community trust

    Three decades of leadership inside the congregations we advise.

Dr. Marilyn White

Co-Founder & Principal

Dr. Marilyn White is a community advocate and nonprofit executive with more than three decades of leadership across the corporate, nonprofit, and faith-based sectors. Before answering the call to ministry, she spent twenty-two years as an executive underwriter in commercial insurance, where she built her expertise in business management, strategic planning, risk analysis, and organisational leadership.

Today she serves as Pastor of St. James United Methodist Church in Houston’s historic Fourth Ward, Freedmen’s Town, and as Executive Director of the Trinity East Village CDC in Third Ward, leading initiatives that strengthen communities through partnership and advocacy. She is widely recognised for turning vision into action, securing more than $1 million in charitable dental services for underserved residents, and providing leadership for the development of a $33 million affordable senior housing community.

She is a retired Elder in Full Connection in the United Methodist Church, and her work reflects a philosophy that pairs compassionate service with sound organisational strategy and measurable community impact.
