How It Works

From Land to Homes

We take churches, community groups, and landowners from a first look at the property through financing, city approvals, and construction. At every stage you get a straight answer about what your land can actually support.

  1. Step 01

    Feasibility & Vision

    We start with the land. What it can carry, what it is worth, and whether the numbers support housing at all, answered before you spend anything.

    • Review the property, title, and site constraints
    • Model AMI tiers and unit mix against real revenue

  2. Step 02

    Financing & Structure

    Then we find the money and the shape of the deal, and help you choose the developer who will build it.

    • Map LIHTC, city, HUD, and private capital pathways
    • Vet and select your developer and vendors

  3. Step 03

    Permitting Support & Build

    Permitting stays with your development team. When issues come up, we help you weigh the options and make the call, then stay with the project until the doors open.

    • Guide decisions when permitting issues arise
    • Monitor construction through to project close
